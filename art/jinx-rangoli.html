Jinx Rangoli

2018

As a dare, I gave my friends a few options for what rangoli I should draw and this won the polls. I ran out of white powder and had to use flour to finish it. Obviously it's absurd, but I think it's more funny that someone put this much time and effort into something so stupid — and had to erase it before his parents woke up.
