Hex Radius

The picture shows a regular hexagon with in-radius $1$. Inside it, shade the region of points that are closer to the centre than to any of the six sides.

What is the area of this region?

Solution

By symmetry, work with one of the six congruent slices. Place the centre at the origin; the top side lies along $y = 1$. The locus of points equidistant from the origin and from this line satisfies $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = |y - 1|$, which simplifies to the parabola

$$y = \tfrac{1}{2}(1 - x^{2}).$$

This parabola intersects the two bounding rays of the slice (lines through the origin at $\pm 30^\circ$ from the vertical) at $x = \pm(2 - \sqrt{3})$. The full area is $12$ copies of the integral of $\tfrac{1}{2}(1 - x^2) - x\sqrt{3}$ from $0$ to $2 - \sqrt{3}$, which evaluates to

$$2(16 - 9\sqrt{3}) \approx 0.8231.$$

Answer: $2(16 - 9\sqrt{3}) \approx 0.8230$
