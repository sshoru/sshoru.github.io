Sequence of Sequences

Build a number triangle as follows: place the positive integers $1, 2, 3, \ldots$ along a down-and-right diagonal of an infinite grid, then fill every other cell by summing the entry directly above and the one directly to the right.

The leftmost column of this triangle turns out to be

$$1,\ 3,\ 8,\ 20,\ 48,\ 112,\ 256,\ 576,\ \ldots$$

Let $S(x) = x + 3x^2 + 8x^3 + 20x^4 + 48x^5 + 112x^6 + 256x^7 + 576x^8 + \cdots$ be the generating function of this column. Evaluate $S\!\left(\tfrac{1}{3}\right)$.

Solution

A bit of counting shows that the $n$-th term in the column equals $\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(k+1)$ — each integer $k+1$ contributes once for every lattice path from its diagonal cell to the leftmost column. Simplifying gives

$$a_n = n\,2^{n-1} + 2^n = \tfrac{1}{2}(n+2)\,2^n.$$

Plugging into the generating function and summing the resulting standard series yields the closed form

$$S(x) = \frac{x(1-x)}{(1-2x)^2},$$

valid for $|x| < \tfrac{1}{2}$. Substituting $x = \tfrac{1}{3}$ gives $S\!\left(\tfrac{1}{3}\right) = \dfrac{(1/3)(2/3)}{(1/3)^2} = 2$.

Answer: $2$
