Controls

Use the joystick to sling the active ion. When in the air, click left mouse again to attach to an interactable object.

Rules

  • The red ion should avoid blue and purple crosses.
  • The blue ion should avoid red and purple crosses.
  • Don't let the ions get too far apart, or they'll break.

My entry for the GMTK Game Jam 2021 — theme: "Joined Together".
