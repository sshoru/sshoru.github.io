I am an Operations Research Scientist at Lyric, working in the Network Science Department.

I hold a Master's in Computer Science from Chennai Mathematical Institute, where I was advised by Prof. M.K. Srivas and Prof. M. Praveen. My interests sit at the intersection of optimization, statistics, formal methods, and machine learning.

In Summer 2025 I worked with Prof. Toshiaki Aoki at JAIST on formal safety verification of autonomous vehicles. A paper from this work was accepted to ACM SAC 2026 (Software Verification and Testing track). In Summer 2024 I was a research intern at LiVe Lab (Masaryk University & TU Munich) under Prof. Jan Křetínský, where we studied LTL synthesis with ML heuristics for winning policies.
